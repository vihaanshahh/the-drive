# Third-party assets

## Selected soundtrack

The game bundles audio from the two Suno tracks selected by the project owner:

- **Thavil Tavil Fusion**: https://suno.com/s/awxWvqNGmejigyCF (song `cc6da657-0978-4a2c-8e5b-bdbffc06f4a3`).
- **chant**: https://suno.com/s/YN3fYb6CP3OXGpWA (song `b2aa13cd-4ac2-4db7-ba5d-40696188b172`).

Audio was extracted from each song's public Suno CDN video download and prepared as stereo 48 kHz AAC in `TheDrive/Assets/Music`. The selected percussion grid is normalized to a shared 97 BPM with pitch preserved, quiet intros/outros are trimmed, and loudness is matched with headroom for crossfades. The runtime plays seeded bar-length excerpts rather than waiting for either song to finish. Source and preparation details are retained alongside the music assets. These songs retain their respective rights; the CC0 character license below does not apply to the soundtrack.

## Gujarat characters

The project owner supplied `Gujarat_Desert_Characters_Pack.zip`, **Gujarat Desert Adventure — Version 2**, containing the driver, explorer, and grandpa. The supplied credits attribute the anatomical foundation to **Blender Studio and community contributors, Human Base Meshes v1.4.1 (CC0)**. The pack's clothing, hair, equipment, materials, and basic body rigs were authored for this project.

- Source anatomy: https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/
- Original pack credits and README: `TheDrive/Assets/Gujarat/SOURCE_ASSET_CREDITS.txt` and `SOURCE_README.txt`.
- Reproducible conversion: `Tools/import_gujarat_characters.py`.
- Source and output checksums, preparation details: `TheDrive/Assets/Gujarat/PROVENANCE.json`.

The dense presentation models are decimated offline, with excessive disconnected fine hair strands thinned deterministically. Original skin influences are normalized to four per vertex and all 22 joints are retained. Authored textile UVs and material colors are packed into 1024-pixel PBR atlases, with small transparent eye/goggle meshes kept separate. The game adds driving, transfer, and walking poses to the supplied FK rigs. The driver's source goggles are moved from the scarf to the face for the existing live road-reflection feature. No source `.blend` or `.glb` files are bundled in the app.

## Earlier character fallback

The humanoid character mesh and skin/eye textures are adapted from **Superhero Male** in **Universal Base Characters — Standard** by **Quaternius**.

- Official source: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
- Official download: https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Original license is retained in `TheDrive/Assets/QUATERNIUS_LICENSE.txt`.

The selected glTF geometry was converted to the compact `DriverMesh.json` manifest and `DriverMesh.bin` vertex/index/skin-weight buffers. UVs, the humanoid hierarchy, joint weights and inverse-bind matrices are preserved. One Loop subdivision smooths the body so limbs and clothing read as a continuous figure; the original skeleton still drives it. Unused UV sets, vertex colors, source animation metadata and unselected models were omitted. Skin and eye textures were resized to 512 and 128 pixels. Clothing regions are assigned game materials; reversible driving-to-gunner animation is authored in Swift using the original skeleton. These are game-ready stylized characters with human proportions, not photorealistic scans.

The terrain, particles, and environment lighting are generated by this project’s code. The previous smoothed Quaternius character remains available as a fallback when a Gujarat asset cannot load. Houses, rocks, the jeep, the rally car, the SUV, and the pursuit bike are original surfaced models.

## App artwork

The app icon and landscape launch illustration are original imagegen outputs created for this project. Their exact prompts, tool choice, and workspace paths are retained in `Tools/ArtDirection.txt`. The displayed title and loading indicator are native UI rather than text baked into the artwork.

## Vehicles

The rally car, pursuing SUV, pursuit bike, and player jeep are original models sized to the road. Wheels are separate meshes centered on their axles so the existing steering and spin can drive them. Glass is a second mesh. Working headlights and the animated occupants are added in Swift. Vertex colors are stored in linear light. These are stylized game models, not photorealistic scans.

`KENNEY_LICENSE.txt` and `VehicleColor.png` remain from an earlier car kit and are not sampled by the current meshes.

## Town architecture

Houses, porch columns, chimneys, fences, the open gate, and roadside rocks are original surfaced meshes in `EnvironmentMesh.json` and `EnvironmentMesh.bin`. Walls have thickness and window openings. Roofs are gables, hips, flat roofs, or street-facing gables, and ruined variants drop a section of roof. Window faces are a separate mesh so they can glow at night. Vertex colors are stored in linear light. The same six lot names and fence names are shared by every seeded district.

The asphalt, shoulder terrain, paint ribbons and tire-contact simulation use the same analytic `RoadSurface` height field. Roads are generated as subdivided meshes rather than flat boxes. Building foundations sample both footprint ends so their bases meet the sloping terrain.
