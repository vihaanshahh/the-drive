ANATOMICAL FOUNDATION — VERSION 2

Blender Studio and community contributors, Human Base Meshes v1.4.1.
The official asset bundle is published under CC0.

Official asset page:
https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/

Official bundle download:
https://download.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/human-base-meshes-bundle-v1.4.1.zip

Asset bundle documentation:
https://developer.blender.org/docs/release_notes/3.6/asset_bundles/

The character anatomy is derived from the realistic anatomical assets in this
bundle, with custom fitting, material treatment, hairstyles, clothing,
accessories, basic body rigging and scene setup for this project.

The earlier version used simplified procedural anatomy. Version 2 replaces
that anatomical foundation rather than treating it as a finished sculpt.
